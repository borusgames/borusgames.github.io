Beat Bloom - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Beat Bloom ("the game") is made by Borus Games. This policy explains what information is used when you play the game and why.

Information we collect

Borus Games does not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information. Your game progress (levels, coins, boosters, settings and the pictures in your album) is saved only on your own device.

Advertising

The game is free to play and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and its partner ad networks (for example Unity Ads). To show and measure ads, these companies may collect information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and how you interact with ads. This information is handled under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • LevelPlay (ironSource): ironSource Mobile privacy policy

On Android, you can choose personalised or non-personalised ads when you first open the game and change this at any time in Settings. On iPhone and iPad, ads are personalised only if you allow tracking when Apple asks; you can change this in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Buying "No Ads" removes the ads between levels; ads you choose to watch for rewards stay available.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Borus Games never receives your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and Borus Games does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Because your progress is stored only on your device, uninstalling the game deletes it. To ask an ad partner to delete data it holds, use the links above or reset your device's advertising ID.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
