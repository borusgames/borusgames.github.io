Rewind – Privacy Policy

Borus Games · Effective 25 November 2026

In short: Rewind has no accounts and we don't collect your name, email or contacts. Your progress, coins and purchases stay on your device. The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, and our ad partners collect some device data to deliver and measure those ads. In-app purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Who we are

Rewind ("the game") is a mobile puzzle game published by Borus Games Ltd ("we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17581838). This policy explains what information is collected when you play Rewind and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, unlocked worlds), your coins, hints, extra changes and slow-mo boosters, the items you have bought, and your settings (music and sounds) are saved only on your device. We do not receive or store this information on any server. Uninstalling the game deletes it; items bought with real money can be restored from the settings screen.

Advertising

Rewind is free to play and is supported by ads, served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with, such as Unity Ads. Ads are optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example a free hint or double coins) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and personalise them, these partners may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or region level);
  • how you interact with ads, such as views, clicks and whether a rewarded ad was watched to the end.

We do not sell your personal information. Our ad partners process this data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Privacy Policy (LevelPlay and Unity Ads)

Your choices

  • Advertising ID: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads). If you opt out, you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your interests.
  • Optional ads: rewarded videos only play if you choose to watch them.
  • No ads: buying "No Ads" or joining the Rewind Club turns off the ads between levels.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

Rewind offers optional in-app purchases (such as coins, hints, boosters, worlds and styles) and an optional auto-renewing subscription, the Rewind Club. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, so the game can give you the item.

The Rewind Club renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. You can manage or cancel it at any time in Google Play (Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data ourselves, the quickest way to exercise these rights over ad data is through the ad partner's privacy page above, or by changing your device settings. You can also email us at borusenterprises@gmail.com and we will help.

Children

Rewind is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will work with our ad partners to remove it.

Security

We use reputable, industry-standard partners for advertising and payments. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: contact Borus Games at borusenterprises@gmail.com.
