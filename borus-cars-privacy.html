In short

  • Borus Cars has no accounts. Your coins, cars, paint jobs, upgrades and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • The game is free and shows ads. Our ad partners receive your device's advertising ID and basic device information to show and measure ads.
  • You choose on first launch whether ads may be personalised, and you can change it any time in Settings → Personalised ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card details.

Who we are

Borus Cars is made by Ans, publishing as Borus Games ("we", "us"). We are responsible for this app and this policy.

What is collected

Stored on your device only

Game progress (coins, owned cars, paint, upgrades, boosters, best lap times, daily-reward streak) and your settings (sound, graphics, controls, speed units, ad preference). This never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Ads

Borus Cars shows two kinds of ads: optional reward videos you choose to watch (for example to double your race coins), and short full-screen ads between races. There are no ads during a race. Buying Remove Ads or the Starter Pack turns off the between-race ads; reward videos stay available if you want them.

If you allow personalised ads, partners may use your advertising ID to show ads based on your interests. If you don't, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests. On iOS, the app only uses your IDFA if you allow tracking when asked.

Read our partners' policies: Unity player privacy policy · ironSource privacy policy.

Purchases

Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and coin packs are bought through Google Play or the App Store, which handle payment and receipts. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack are one-time purchases and can be restored with Restore Purchases in the coin store. Coins are a virtual in-game currency with no cash value; they can't be exchanged for money or transferred.

Your choices

  • Personalised ads: switch on or off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Game data: use Reset Progress in Settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

Borus Cars is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected by our ad partners, contact us and we will help you request deletion.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the UK, EU or California) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to its use for personalised ads, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, most requests are best made to our ad partners using the links above; we are happy to help with that.
