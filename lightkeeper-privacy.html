Lightkeeper: Lost Boats – Privacy Policy

Borus Games · Effective 27 November 2026

In short: Lightkeeper has no accounts and we don't collect your name, email or contacts. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, and our ad partners collect some device data to deliver and measure those ads. The first time ads are switched on, the game asks whether ads may be personalised, and you can change that at any time in Settings. Optional purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details.

Who we are

Lightkeeper: Lost Boats ("the game") is a mobile game published by Borus Games ("we", "us"), the trading name of Borus Games Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17581838). This policy explains what information is collected when you play the game and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (nights completed, stars, coins, upgrades, beam colours, lighthouse styles and bottles found) and your settings (sound, music and your ad choice) are saved only on your device. We do not receive or store this information on any server. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free to play and is supported by ads, served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with, such as Unity Ads. Ads are optional rewards (for example doubling the coins from a night, or a second chance in Long Night) and occasional full-screen ads between nights. To show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and (if you allow it) personalise them, these partners may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where allowed);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or region level);
  • how you interact with ads, such as views, clicks and whether a rewarded ad was watched to the end.

We do not sell your personal information. Our ad partners process this data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Privacy Policy (LevelPlay and Unity Ads)

In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases (Remove Ads and coin packs). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can deliver it in the game.

Links to our other games

The game includes postcards for other Borus Games titles. Tapping one opens that game's page in Google Play or the App Store. No personal information is passed to us when you do this.

Your choices

  • Personalised ads: the first time ads are available, the game asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change this any time in Settings (Personalised ads). If you say no, you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your interests.
  • Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads switches off full-screen ads. Rewarded ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads; on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data ourselves, the quickest way to exercise these rights over ad data is through the ad partner's privacy page above, or by changing your choices in the game and your device settings. You can also email us at borusenterprises@gmail.com and we will help.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will work with our ad partners to remove it.

Security

We use reputable, industry-standard partners for advertising and payments. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: contact Borus Games at borusenterprises@gmail.com.
