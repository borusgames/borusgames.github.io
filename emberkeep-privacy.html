Emberkeep – Privacy Policy

Borus Games · Effective 21 November 2026

In short: Emberkeep has no accounts and we don't collect your name, email or contacts. Your game progress stays on your device. The game may show ads through Unity LevelPlay, and our ad partners collect some device data to deliver and measure those ads. The first time ads are switched on, the game asks whether ads may be personalised, and you can change that at any time from the pause menu.

Who we are

Emberkeep ("the game") is a mobile tower defense game published by Borus Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play Emberkeep and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (stars, unlocked kingdoms and realms, Ember Tree upgrades and quality setting) and your settings (sound and your ad choice) are saved only on your device. We do not receive or store this information on any server. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

Emberkeep is free to play and may be supported by ads, served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with, such as Unity Ads. Ads are optional rewards (for example a second chance after losing a level, or extra embers before the first wave) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and (if you allow it) personalise them, these partners may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or region level);
  • how you interact with ads, such as views, clicks and whether a rewarded ad was watched to the end.

We do not sell your personal information. Our ad partners process this data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Privacy Policy (LevelPlay and Unity Ads)

Your choices

  • Personalised ads: the first time ads are available, Emberkeep asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change this any time from the pause menu (Ad choice). If you say no, you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your interests.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Optional ads: rewarded ads only play if you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases

Emberkeep currently has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added, payments will be handled entirely by Google Play, and we will never see or store your payment details. This policy will be updated before such an update is released.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data ourselves, the quickest way to exercise these rights over ad data is through the ad partner's privacy page above, or by changing your choices in the game and your device settings. You can also email us at borusenterprises@gmail.com and we will help.

Children

Emberkeep is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will work with our ad partners to remove it.

Security

We use reputable, industry-standard partners for advertising. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: contact Borus Games at borusenterprises@gmail.com.
