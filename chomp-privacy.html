Chomp City – Privacy Policy

Borus Games · Effective 11 November 2026

In short: Chomp City has no accounts and we don't collect your name, email or contacts. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, and our ad partners collect some device data to deliver and measure those ads. You choose whether ads can be personalised (on Android in the game's Settings, on iPhone and iPad through Apple's tracking permission), and you can change that at any time.

Who we are

Chomp City ("the game") is a mobile arcade game published by Borus Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play Chomp City and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (level, coins, upgrades, Big Starts, unlocked monsters, daily reward streak) and your settings (sound, music, vibration and your ad choice) are saved only on your device. We do not receive or store this information on any server. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

Chomp City is free to play and is supported by ads, served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with, such as Unity Ads. To show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and (if you allow it) personalise them, these partners may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID, or on iPhone and iPad the Identifier for Advertisers, which is only shared if you allow tracking);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or region level);
  • how you interact with ads, such as views, clicks and whether a rewarded ad was watched to the end.

We do not sell your personal information. Our ad partners process this data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Privacy Policy (LevelPlay and Unity Ads)

Your choices

  • Personalised ads on Android: the first time you open Chomp City, we ask whether ads may be personalised. You can change this any time in the game's Settings → Personal Ads. If you say no, you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your interests.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad: before any ads load, iOS asks whether Chomp City may track you across other companies' apps and websites. Ads are only personalised if you tap "Allow". If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised. You can change this any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads; on iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising).
  • Optional ads: rewarded ads (for example a revive, extra time, a Big Start, free coins or double coins) only play if you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases

Chomp City offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs and Remove Ads). Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. A record of your purchases is kept by the store so that Remove Ads can be restored on a new device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data ourselves, the quickest way to exercise these rights over ad data is through the ad partner's privacy page above, or by changing your choices in the game and your device settings. You can also email us at borusenterprises@gmail.com and we will help.

Children

Chomp City is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will work with our ad partners to remove it.

Security

We use reputable, industry-standard partners for advertising. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: contact Borus Games at borusenterprises@gmail.com.
